About the practice

I'm a designer specialising in poster art and exhibition graphics, based in Manchester. My work sits at the intersection of print and digital, using bold colour, layered typography and constructed imagery to communicate ideas with clarity and emotional weight.

Across a decade of practice I've produced posters for cultural institutions, independent publishers, music and performance, and launched a small press of my own. The portfolio here spans that range, with each piece accompanied by a brief note on intent and process.
