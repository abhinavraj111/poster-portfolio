About the practice

I'm Abhinav Raj, a designer specialising in poster art and editorial graphics, based in Manchester. My work sits at the intersection of print and digital, using bold colour, layered typography and constructed imagery to communicate ideas with clarity and emotional weight.

Each poster here is a standalone research piece: the composition, colour system and type hierarchy are all built around a single idea, then resolved for both screen and scaled print. The work ranges from neon-lit cityscapes to character-driven minimalism, and each piece is designed to be legible as a small thumbnail and as a large-format wall piece in equal measure.
